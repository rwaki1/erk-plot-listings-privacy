Privacy Notice

Updated 17 November 2026

Who operates this service

ERK operates Plot Listings. Contact etienne.rwakineza@gmail.com for privacy questions, corrections or deletion requests.

Information we collect

When you send a plot listing to our designated WhatsApp business number, we receive your sender phone number, message identifier, message text, timestamp and supported image captions or media identifiers. Listings may include location, plot size, UPI, owner contact details and pictures. Share only information you are authorized to provide.

Why we use it

We extract and organize plot listings, identify duplicates using UPI, retain sources and flag conflicting details for review. Property and ownership claims are not independently verified by this service.

Access and service providers

The current prototype stores messages and listing records in a local database and exposes them through an authenticated API to authorized operators. It does not offer a public listings API. WhatsApp delivery uses Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. During testing, Cloudflare Tunnel carries webhook requests to the local service. These providers process data under their applicable terms and privacy notices.

This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as visitor IP addresses when serving this page. This page contains no analytics scripts or contact forms.

Storage and retention

Signed incoming webhook payloads and extracted records are retained in the database. The prototype does not currently apply automatic expiry to stored records. Records remain until an authorized operator removes them; requests for deletion are handled manually using the contact below. Image downloads and durable picture storage are not yet implemented in the prototype. Access is restricted to authorized operators; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your requests

Contact etienne.rwakineza@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of information relating to you. We may need enough information to identify the relevant record and verify your request. Requests are handled manually in this prototype.

Changes

We will update this notice when the service, providers or data practices change.
